How to use a weekly hourly planner

A weekly hourly schedule template shows the whole week with times, so you can see where every hour goes before the week starts.

  1. Write up to four priorities in the strip, and the dates next to each day.
  2. Block fixed commitments. Meetings, classes, shifts and appointments go into their hours first.
  3. Give each priority its hours. Mornings usually suit focused work, so start there.
  4. Leave an open hour each day. Something always runs over.
  5. Review on Sunday. Tick off the priorities you finished and carry the rest to next week.

Frequently asked questions

Is the weekly hourly planner free?

Yes. The weekly hourly planner PDF is completely free. There is no signup and no email required. Click the download link and print a sheet for every week.

What hours does the weekly hourly planner cover?

One row per hour from 6 AM to 10 PM, every day from Monday to Sunday, with a light dashed line at each half hour so you can start or end a block on the half.

How is it different from the 7-day weekly template and the 2027 weekly planner?

The 7-day weekly time blocking template is a portrait page with every hour split into 15-minute rows. The 2027 weekly planner is dated and keeps its priorities and notes in a side panel. This weekly hourly planner is undated and landscape: the priorities strip runs across the top so the seven day columns get the full page width, and every cell repeats its hour, so you can read a time anywhere on the grid without looking back at the time column.

Can I print the weekly hourly planner on US Letter paper?

Yes. The page is A4 landscape with at least 13 mm of white space on every side, so it prints on US Letter at Actual Size without clipping. If your printer still cuts an edge, choose "Fit to printable area" (or "Fit to page") in the print dialog.

Can I use the weekly hourly planner on my iPad?

Yes. Open the PDF in GoodNotes, Notability, or any PDF annotation app and write on it with your Apple Pencil or finger.
