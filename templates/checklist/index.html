Two checklists for two kinds of job

Some lists you work through once: packing for a trip, moving house, getting a room ready for a guest. Page 1 is for those. Write the job on the Title line, list the items, and tick until nothing is left.

Other lists repeat every day: watering the plants, checking tomorrow's calendar, clearing the kitchen. Writing them out again each morning is a waste. Page 2 is a daily checklist you fill in once a week: the items go down the side, and each day gets its own column of boxes.

If your daily list is really a routine with a time to finish by, the morning routine checklist gives each step its minutes and a running clock time.

How to make a checklist

  1. Name the job on the Title line and write the date.
  2. List the items in order, one per line. If the job has steps, write them in the order you'll do them, so the list can be worked top to bottom.
  3. Tick as you go. Whatever is unticked at the end is what's left.
  4. For the daily checklist, write the everyday items down the side of page 2 once, and the dates under the days.
  5. Tick each day's column and write the count on the Done today line. Start a fresh sheet on Monday.

Frequently asked questions

Is the checklist template free?

Yes. The checklist PDF is completely free. There is no signup and no email required. Download it and print as many copies as you need.

What is in the checklist PDF?

Two A4 pages. Page 1 is a blank checklist: a Title and a Date line, then 30 numbered items, each with a check box and a writing line. Page 2 is a daily checklist for one week: 15 item rows down the side, a check box for each day from Monday to Sunday, and a Done today count under every day.

How does the daily checklist page work?

Write the things you do every day down the side once and the dates under the days. Each day, tick the box in that day's column when an item is done, and write how many you ticked on the Done today line. One sheet covers a week.

How is it different from the morning routine checklist?

The morning routine checklist is one routine in a fixed order, where each step has its minutes and a running clock time that adds up to a leave-the-house time. The checklist template has no times at all: page 1 is a plain list of up to 30 items for any job, and page 2 repeats 15 items across a whole week. Choose the morning routine checklist to time a routine, and this one to keep track of what's done.

What can I use the blank checklist for?

Anything with steps or items to remember: packing for a trip, moving house, a new hire's first week, a monthly closing routine at work, or a party shopping list. Write the job on the Title line and one item per line.

Can I print it on US Letter paper?

Yes. Both pages are A4 portrait with wide margins, so they print on US Letter too. Choose "Fit to page" in the print dialog if your printer adds its own margins.
