How to plan 2027 in an Excel calendar

  1. Save a copy named for the year, and keep the download as a blank spare.
  2. Color the year first. On the 2027 sheet, give trips, school breaks and launches a fill color.
  3. Fill in each month before it starts. Type appointments and deadlines into the cell under their date.
  4. Set a focus. One line next to Focus this month, and one line per week in the Notes column.
  5. Print the month if you want it on the fridge or the desk: each sheet fits one landscape page.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Excel calendar template free?

Yes. The 2027 Excel calendar template is completely free. There is no signup and no email required. Download the .xlsx and open it in Excel or Numbers.

What's in the 2027 Excel calendar?

Thirteen sheets. The 2027 sheet shows the whole year in three rows of four months with the federal holidays highlighted and listed below. Then one sheet per month, January to December, with weeks starting on Sunday, a large cell under each date, a Notes column for every week and a Focus this month field.

Which holidays are filled in?

The eleven US federal holidays of 2027, each in its date cell. When a holiday falls on a weekend, the weekday it is observed is marked too: Juneteenth on Friday, June 18, Independence Day on Monday, July 5, and Christmas Day on Friday, December 24. The December sheet also marks Friday, December 31, when New Year's Day 2028 is observed.

How is it different from the printable 2027 calendar?

The 2027 calendar PDF is made for printing and writing by hand: two rows of six months on the year page and mini calendars beside each month. This Excel calendar is made for typing: a writing cell under every date, a notes column beside every week, four months to a row on the year sheet, and frozen weekday headers. It prints too, one page per sheet.

Can I use it as an Excel planner template?

Yes. Use the Focus this month field for the month's goal, the notes column for each week's priorities and the day cells for deadlines and plans. For hours in a day, pair it with the Excel schedule template.
