How to make a weekly cleaning schedule

A cleaning schedule template works when each task sits at the right frequency. Too much in Every day and the list feels endless. Too little and the bathroom waits until Saturday.

  1. Keep Every day short. Four to six tasks that take a few minutes each: beds, dishes, counters, a quick tidy.
  2. Give each weekly task a day. One group per weekday keeps each day to 20 or 30 minutes. Leave one day light or empty for a rest.
  3. Batch the monthly jobs. The oven, the microwave and the dishwasher filter can all go in Once a month.
  4. Put a month next to each seasonal job, so the windows and gutters come round without you having to remember them.
  5. Tick as you go, and print a fresh sheet each week. If you miss a day, skip it and pick up with today's group.

Frequently asked questions

Is the house cleaning schedule free?

Yes. The house cleaning schedule PDF is completely free. There is no signup and no email required. Click the download link and print it.

What does the cleaning schedule template include?

Three bands on one A4 page. Every day: seven task lines, each with a checkbox for Monday to Sunday. Once a week: a box for each weekday with five task lines, plus a notes box. Once a month and seasonal: six lines each, and the seasonal list has a When column for the months. Page 2 is the same sheet filled in as an example.

How does a weekly cleaning schedule split the work?

Give each weekly task its own day instead of cleaning everything on Saturday. On the sheet, Monday might be laundry, Wednesday the bathrooms and Saturday the sheets and floors, so each day takes 20 to 30 minutes and no day takes all afternoon.

How is it different from the morning routine checklist?

The morning routine checklist is one sequence of steps with minutes that add up to a leave-the-house time. The cleaning schedule sorts tasks by how often they come round: every day, once a week on a set day, once a month and a few times a year. If you want a timed routine, use the morning routine checklist instead.

How often should I do each cleaning task?

There is no fixed rule, so start with what gets dirty fastest. Dishes, counters and a quick tidy usually go in Every day, bathrooms, floors, laundry and bed sheets in Once a week, ovens, vents and the fridge in Once a month, and windows, gutters and curtains in Seasonal. Move a task up or down after a few weeks.

Can I print it on US Letter paper?

Yes. Both pages are A4 portrait with wide margins, so they print on US Letter too. Choose "Fit to page" in the print dialog if your printer adds its own margins.
