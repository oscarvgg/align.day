How to use a daily planner

A daily planner template keeps the two halves of a day on one sheet: what you need to do, and when you will do it. A list alone tends to grow; a schedule alone forgets the small tasks. Put them side by side and you can see whether the day fits.

  1. Date the page. Write the date and circle the weekday. The planner is undated, so a skipped day costs nothing.
  2. Pick your Top 3. The three things that would make today a success.
  3. Empty your head into the to-do list. Everything else, small tasks included, one per checkbox.
  4. Schedule the important things. Fixed commitments go into their hours first. Then give each Top 3 item an hour of its own, and batch small to-dos into one slot.
  5. Close the day. Tick off what you finished, write what slipped in For tomorrow, and start tomorrow's page from that box.

Top 3: 1. send the client proposal · 2. book flights for March · 3. 30-minute walk

To do: ☑ reply to Sam about Friday · ☑ pay the electricity bill · ☑ order printer ink · ☐ return the library books · ☑ call the bank · ☐ sign up for the 10K

Three small to-dos share the 1 PM hour, so they don't interrupt the proposal in the morning. The two that didn't happen go into For tomorrow, and that box is the first thing you copy onto the next page.
