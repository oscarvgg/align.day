Why a wedding day schedule needs a who and a where

On a wedding day, a time on its own isn't enough. The photographer needs to know where the first look is, the DJ needs to know when to call the entrance, and your aunt needs to know when family photos start. A row with a time, a who and a where answers all three, and it's the same idea as time blocking: every part of the day gets its own slot, with room around it.

The Who and Where columns are what set this sheet apart from the daily schedule template, which prints half-hour rows from 5 AM to midnight to plan an ordinary day and compare it with what really happened.

How to make a wedding day timeline

  1. Write the ceremony time first and put it in the middle of the page.
  2. Work backwards to getting ready: line-up, photos, getting dressed, hair and makeup. Hair and makeup usually takes longest, so start it early.
  3. Work forwards to the send-off: family photos, cocktail hour, entrance, dinner, toasts, dances, cake and the exit.
  4. Fill in Who and Where for every row. If a row has no who, nobody will make it happen.
  5. Add 10 to 15 minutes of slack before the ceremony and before dinner, where delays pile up.
  6. Check it with your vendors, then give a copy to everyone named in the Who column.

Frequently asked questions

Is the wedding day timeline template free?

Yes. The wedding day timeline template PDF is completely free. There is no signup and no email required. Click the download link and print it.

What's on the two pages?

Page 1 is the blank timeline: Couple, Date and Ceremony fields, 25 rows with Time, What happens, Who and Where, and a contacts box with a name and phone for ten roles, from the coordinator to transport. Page 2 is the same sheet filled in for a 4 PM ceremony, from hair and makeup at 9 AM to a sparkler send-off at 10 PM.

When should I start the wedding day timeline?

Start from the ceremony time and work outwards: what has to happen before it, and in what order after it. Most couples draft it four to six weeks before the wedding, check it with the photographer, venue and caterer, and send the final version to everyone named in the Who column the week of the wedding.

Does it work as a day of timeline template for a planner or coordinator?

Yes. The Who and Where columns tell each person when and where they are needed, and the contacts box puts every vendor's phone number on the same page, so a day-of coordinator can run the day from one sheet.

How is it different from the daily schedule template?

The daily schedule has half-hour rows printed from 5 AM to midnight, with Plan and Actual columns for an ordinary day. The wedding day timeline has no printed times: each row is one moment of the day, with who is needed and where, and the vendor contacts sit on the same page. If you want a printed half-hour grid for a normal day, use the daily schedule template.

Can I print it on US Letter paper?

Yes. Both pages are A4 portrait with wide margins, so they print on US Letter too. Choose "Fit to page" in the print dialog: at actual size, Letter is shorter than A4 and the footer can be clipped.
