How to make a weekly schedule in Excel

  1. Open the workbook and save a copy for the week, so the template stays blank.
  2. Type the fixed times first on the Weekly sheet: classes, shifts, meetings, the school run.
  3. Shade each block with a fill color: select its cells, then use Fill Color on the Home tab in Excel, or the Format sidebar in Numbers. One color per kind of work.
  4. Merge nothing. Leave the half-hour cells separate so you can move a block later by cutting and pasting its cells.
  5. Plan the day on the Daily sheet each morning: copy the day's blocks across, pick your Top 3 and tick Done as you finish.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Excel schedule template free?

Yes. The Excel schedule template is completely free. There is no signup and no email required. Download the .xlsx and open it in Excel or Numbers.

What's in the workbook?

Two sheets. Weekly is an Excel weekly schedule template: Monday to Sunday in half-hour rows from 6 AM to 10 PM, a Week of field and a notes row. Daily is an Excel daily schedule template: the same half-hour rows with a Done column, plus Top 3 priorities, a to-do list and notes beside them.

How is it different from the Excel time blocking template?

The Excel time blocking template has daily, weekly and monthly tabs, and its week runs Monday to Friday. This schedule template runs all seven days in half-hour rows with the times already filled in, freezes the header row and time column while you scroll, and prints each sheet on one page. For daily, weekly and monthly tabs, use the Excel time blocking template.

Does it work in Numbers and on a Mac?

Yes. Excel for Mac and Windows, Excel for iPad and Apple Numbers all open the .xlsx. In Numbers, each sheet becomes its own tab. The file also opens in Google Sheets; the Google Sheets schedule template shows how.

Will it print on one page?

Yes. Each sheet has its print area set and is scaled to fit one page: the weekly sheet in landscape, the daily sheet in portrait. It prints on US Letter or A4. A printable PDF of both sheets is on this page too.
