How to make a class schedule

A class schedule template is a timetable you can read in two seconds between classes, so fill it in once, neatly, at the start of the term.

  1. Write the bell times first. Copy each period's start and end time from your school's schedule into the period column.
  2. List every course with its room and teacher, and give each one a mark (●, ■, ▲) or a shade in the key box.
  3. Fill in the week, one class per cell, with the room in the corner. A class that meets every day takes the whole row.
  4. Mark the classes that change, such as a lab on Tuesdays and Thursdays, with the same key mark so they stand out.
  5. Use the notes box for lunch, early-release days and the dates of your first exams.

Frequently asked questions

Is the class schedule template free?

Yes. The class schedule PDF is completely free. There is no signup and no email required. Click the download link and print it.

What does the class schedule template include?

One A4 landscape page. On the left, a timetable with a column for each day from Monday to Friday and eight numbered period rows, each with a start and end time you write in. On the right, a course list of 10 rows with a key box, the course, the room and the teacher, and a notes box for bell times, A/B days or exam dates.

How is it different from the study schedule template?

The study schedule template plans a whole week, Monday to Sunday, in one-hour rows from 7 AM to 11 PM, with class, study and free blocks. The class schedule is only your timetable: five school days by eight periods, with the room and teacher for each course. Use the class schedule to know where to be, and the study schedule to plan study time around your classes.

Can I use it as a college schedule template?

Yes. Treat each period row as a time slot and write your lecture, seminar and lab times in the period column. A course that meets on Monday, Wednesday and Friday fills the same row on those three days, and the course list keeps each building, room and professor in one place.

My school has fewer than eight periods. Does it still work?

Yes. Fill the periods your school has and leave the rest blank, or use a spare row for a zero period, an after-school class or a lab that only meets on some days.

Can I print the class schedule on US Letter paper?

Yes. The page is A4 landscape with at least 13 mm of white space on every side, so it prints on US Letter at Actual Size without clipping. If your printer still cuts an edge, choose "Fit to page" in the print dialog.
