How to use a digital planner for time blocking

A digital planner works like a paper one, with one advantage: you jump to any month or week with a tap instead of flipping pages.

  1. Start on the month. Tap the month's tab, write up to five goals for the month, and mark fixed dates in their day boxes.
  2. Open the week. Tap the Week button at the left of the week's row to jump to its page and write up to five priorities for the week.
  3. Block fixed commitments first. Classes, meetings, commutes and appointments go into their hours.
  4. Give each priority a block. Put the work that needs focus where you focus best, and spread big tasks across several days.
  5. Review at the end of the week. Tick off what you finished, tap Week › and carry anything that slipped to the next page.

Frequently asked questions

Is the 2027 digital planner free?

Yes. The 2027 digital planner PDF is completely free. There is no signup and no email required. Click the download link and import the file into your note-taking app.

How do I import the digital planner into GoodNotes or Notability?

Download the PDF on your iPad and save it to Files. In GoodNotes, tap New, choose Import and pick the PDF. In Notability, open the library, press and hold + New, choose Import and pick the PDF. You can also open the PDF in Files, tap Share and choose either app. GoodNotes and Notability are not affiliated with Align; the planner is a plain PDF that works in any PDF app.

Do the links inside the PDF work?

Yes. Every page has a row of tabs for 2027 and the twelve months. On the year page, each month name opens its month and each week number or date opens its week. On a month page, the Week button at the left of each row opens that week's page, and every week page links back to its month and to the weeks before and after. Links work in PDF apps that follow links inside a PDF, such as GoodNotes and Notability. If a tap writes instead of jumping, switch the app to its read-only or view mode first.

How is the digital planner different from the 2027 weekly planner?

The digital planner is made for a screen: its pages are 4:3 landscape, sized for iPad, and it adds month tabs on every page, twelve month pages with a goals box, and links between the year, the months and the weeks. The 2027 weekly planner is made for paper: A4 landscape pages with a year page and 53 weeks, and no links or month pages. The week pages share the same 6 AM to 10 PM time-block grid.

Which dates does the 2027 digital planner cover?

All of 2027. It has 53 Monday-to-Sunday week pages, from Monday, December 28, 2026 to Sunday, January 2, 2028, so January 1 and December 31 each sit in a full week. Weeks are numbered 1 to 53 in order and don't follow ISO week numbers.

Can I print the digital planner?

Yes. Each page is 276 x 207 mm with 12 mm margins, so it fits on US Letter and A4 paper in landscape without clipping. Choose "Fit to page" if your printer adds its own margins.

Does the digital planner have daily pages?

No. This version has a year page, twelve month pages and 53 week pages. Each week page gives every day its own column with one row per hour, which is where you plan each day.
