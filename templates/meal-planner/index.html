How to use a weekly meal planner template

The point of planning meals is the shopping list. With both on one page, you write an ingredient down the moment you plan the meal that needs it.

  1. Start with what you have. Plan one or two meals around food that needs using up.
  2. Fill in the dinners first. They take the most shopping and the most time.
  3. Repeat the easy meals. The same two breakfasts all week is a plan, not a failure.
  4. Write the list as you go, in the section where you'll find it in the shop.
  5. Plan the leftovers. Cook extra on Sunday and write Monday's lunch on the prep ahead line.

Frequently asked questions

Is the meal planner template free?

Yes. The meal planner PDF is completely free. There is no signup and no email required. Click the download link and print a sheet for every week.

What does the weekly meal planner template include?

One A4 landscape page: Monday to Sunday columns, each with a line for the date, by Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner and Snacks rows with lines to write in. A shopping list runs down the right side in five sections (fruit and veg; meat, fish and eggs; dairy; pantry; other), each with five checkbox lines. A prep ahead / leftovers line sits under the grid.

How is it different from the weekly planner?

The weekly planner is a portrait page of lined day boxes for any plans, with a top-3 box and a to-do list. The meal planner is only for food: every day split into four meals, and a shopping list next to the week so you can write each ingredient down as you plan the meal. For appointments and to-dos, use the weekly planner instead.

How do I plan meals for the week?

Start with dinners, since they take the most shopping and cooking. Check what's already in the fridge, plan one or two meals that use up leftovers, and repeat easy breakfasts and lunches. Then go through the grid and write every ingredient you don't have in the shopping list.

Does the meal planner count calories or nutrition?

No. The sheet has no calorie, macro or nutrition fields. It plans what you'll eat and what you need to buy. Use the cells however you like, for recipes, page numbers or who's cooking.

Can I print the meal planner on US Letter paper?

Yes. The page is A4 landscape with at least 13 mm of white space on every side, so it prints on US Letter at Actual Size without clipping. If your printer still cuts an edge, choose "Fit to page" in the print dialog.
