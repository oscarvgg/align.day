Why the clock column matters

A plain checklist tells you what to do. It doesn't tell you whether it fits. Here every step's minutes push the clock forward, so the last line is the time you'll actually leave. If that's later than your Leave by time, you find out on paper, not at the door.

The clock column is also what sets this sheet apart from the time boxing template, which gives separate tasks their own box of minutes and a start and an end. This sheet is one routine in a fixed order, adding up to one time. For housework that comes round daily, weekly or monthly, use the house cleaning schedule.

For a plain list with no times, or the same items ticked off every day of the week, use the checklist template.

How to make a morning routine checklist

  1. Write the two times at the top: when you wake up and when you need to leave.
  2. List the steps in order, one per row, from getting up to walking out of the door. Small steps count: finding keys takes minutes too.
  3. Write the minutes each step really takes.
  4. Run the clock. The first clock time is your wake-up time. Add each step's minutes to get the next clock time, down to the Leave the house line.
  5. Check against Leave by. If it doesn't fit, cut or shorten a step, or set the wake-up time earlier. Then use the Done boxes each morning.
