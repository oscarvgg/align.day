How to use a weekly planning template

A weekly plan template works best when you fill it in once, before the week starts, in about ten minutes.

  1. Write the week's dates on the Week of line and next to each day.
  2. Pick this week's top 3. If you only finished these, would the week feel like a success?
  3. Empty your head into the to-do list. Calls, errands, small tasks: everything, without deciding when.
  4. Fill the day boxes. Write fixed appointments first, then give each top 3 item and each to-do a day. Leave Friday light for what slips.
  5. Review on Sunday. Tick off what you finished and move the rest to the Next week box.

Frequently asked questions

Is the weekly planner template free?

Yes. The weekly planner PDF is completely free. There is no signup and no email required. Click the download link and print a sheet for every week.

Is the weekly planner dated?

No. It is undated, so you can start any week and skip weeks without wasting pages. Write the dates on the Week of line and next to each day. If you want every week of 2027 already dated, use the 2027 weekly planner.

How is it different from the other weekly templates?

The weekly time blocking templates and the 2027 weekly planner are hour-by-hour grids. This weekly planner has no time grid at all: each day is a lined box for tasks and appointments, with a top 3 box, a to-do list and a notes / next week strip on the same portrait page.

Can I print the weekly planner on US Letter paper?

Yes. The page is A4 portrait with at least 12 mm of white space on every side (about 15 mm at the sides), so it prints on US Letter at Actual Size without clipping. If your printer still cuts an edge, choose "Fit to printable area" (or "Fit to page") in the print dialog.

Can I use the weekly planner on my iPad?

Yes. Open the PDF in GoodNotes, Notability, or any PDF annotation app and write on it with your Apple Pencil or finger.
