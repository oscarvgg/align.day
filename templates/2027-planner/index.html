US federal holidays in 2027

  • New Year's Day: Friday, January 1
  • Martin Luther King Jr. Day: Monday, January 18
  • Washington's Birthday: Monday, February 15
  • Memorial Day: Monday, May 31
  • Juneteenth: Saturday, June 19 (observed Friday, June 18)
  • Independence Day: Sunday, July 4 (observed Monday, July 5)
  • Labor Day: Monday, September 6
  • Columbus Day: Monday, October 11
  • Veterans Day: Thursday, November 11
  • Thanksgiving Day: Thursday, November 25
  • Christmas Day: Saturday, December 25 (observed Friday, December 24)

A federal holiday that falls on a Saturday is observed on the Friday before, and one on a Sunday on the Monday after. The planner marks both the official and the observed day for each holiday dated in 2027. New Year's Day 2028 falls on a Saturday, so it is observed on Friday, December 31, 2027, which the planner leaves blank: write it in if you plan a year-end closure.

How to plan 2027 month by month

A calendar shows what is already fixed. A planner decides what the month is for. Spend ten minutes on the plan page before each month starts, and the month grid next to it shows when the work can happen.

  1. Mark the year's fixed dates. Circle trips, school breaks, launches and family events on the year at a glance.
  2. Pick the month's top 3. Write the three results that would make the month a good one. Three is enough to finish; ten is a wish list.
  3. Fill in the key dates and the grid. Add deadlines, appointments and birthdays to Key dates, then write each in its day box.
  4. List the to-dos. Break each top-3 result into tasks, and give the biggest ones a day on the grid.
  5. Plan each week. Each Sunday, move the week's tasks and dates into a weekly planner and give them their hours.
  6. Review at the end of the month. Tick what got done, write what you learned in Notes, and carry what slipped to next month's plan page.
