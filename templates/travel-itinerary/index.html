Why a day-by-day itinerary works

A trip has a few fixed times, a flight, a check-in, a dinner booking, and a lot of open hours between them. Writing the fixed times first and planning around them is time blocking: each part of the day gets its own slot, with travel time between places. The day page has no printed hours on purpose, so a day that starts with a 6 AM train and one that starts with a late brunch both fit.

The trip overview and the Place column are what set this sheet apart from the hourly planner, which prints one row per hour from 6 AM to 11 PM for an ordinary day at home.

How to plan a trip with this template

  1. Print page 1 once and page 2 once for each day.
  2. Fill in the overview: destination, travelers, an emergency contact, then every flight, train or rental car with its times and confirmation number.
  3. Add stays and key bookings, each with its confirmation number, so you never search your inbox at a check-in desk.
  4. Sketch the trip day by day: one line per day with where you are and what the day is about.
  5. Plan each day page. Write the bookings first, then fill the gaps with activities and places. Leave time to get from one place to the next.
  6. Take the day's page with you each morning, and tick off the Don't forget box before you leave.

Frequently asked questions

Is the travel itinerary template free?

Yes. The travel itinerary template PDF is completely free. There is no signup and no email required. Click the download link and print it.

What's on the two pages?

Page 1 is the trip overview: destination, travelers, an emergency contact, and tables for getting there and around, where you stay, key bookings, and a one-line plan for up to ten days. Page 2 is a day page with 22 rows of Time, Activity, Place and Notes, a Staying tonight line and a Don't forget box. Print page 1 once and page 2 once for each day of the trip.

Can I use it as a vacation itinerary template or a daily itinerary?

Yes. For a vacation, fill in the overview and print a day page for each day you want to plan. For a single busy day, such as a day trip or a city layover, print just the day page.

How is it different from the hourly planner?

The hourly planner has the hours printed down the side for an ordinary day at home. The travel itinerary leaves the time blank, so you write only the times that matter, and every row has a Place column, because on a trip where you are matters as much as when. It also has the trip overview page, which no other template here has. If you want printed hours for a normal day, use the hourly planner.

Can I print it on US Letter paper?

Yes. Both pages are A4 portrait with wide margins, so they print on US Letter too. Choose "Fit to page" in the print dialog: at actual size, Letter is shorter than A4 and the footer can be clipped.
