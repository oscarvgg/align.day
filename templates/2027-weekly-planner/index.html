How to use a 2027 weekly planner for time blocking

A weekly time blocking planner turns a to-do list into a plan: every task gets its own block of hours on a specific day before the week begins.

  1. Write this week's priorities. Pick up to five things that would make the week a success.
  2. Block fixed commitments first. Meetings, classes, commutes, meals and appointments go into their hours.
  3. Give each priority a block. Put deep work in the mornings if that is when you focus best, and spread big tasks across several days.
  4. Leave some hours open. A week planned to the hour breaks at the first surprise; open blocks absorb what runs over.
  5. Review on Sunday. Tick off what you finished and carry anything that slipped to next week's page.
