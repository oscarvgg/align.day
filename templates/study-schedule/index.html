How to make a weekly study schedule

  1. List this week's exams and deadlines in the box on the left, with the day each one falls on.
  2. Write in your classes first. Lectures, seminars and labs are fixed, so draw a solid box around each one before planning anything else.
  3. Add study blocks around the classes and draw a dashed box around each one: short reviews on class days, longer blocks for assignments.
  4. Keep free time on the page. Draw a dotted box around a free evening and part of the weekend.
  5. Check the week on Sunday. Tick off what's done and carry the rest onto next week's sheet.

The week follows four rules:

  1. Classes go in first. They can't move, so every other block is planned around them.
  2. Reviews are short and come soon after class. One hour on the same day, while the notes are still in your bag, rather than one long catch-up session at the weekend.
  3. Bigger work gets longer blocks, planned back from the deadline. The problem set is split across Monday and Wednesday so it's finished before Thursday, and the essay starts on Tuesday so Sunday is only for finishing it.
  4. Free time is written in. Friday evening and most of the weekend are marked Free, so they're part of the plan rather than whatever is left over.
