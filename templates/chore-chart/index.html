How to make a chore chart that gets used

A chore chart template only works if everyone can read it at a glance and knows what a blank box means.

  1. List the chores first, then share them out. Daily jobs at the top, weekly jobs below.
  2. Write one name per chore in the Who column. Two names on one chore usually means nobody does it.
  3. Shade the days a chore isn't due, so a blank box always means "not done yet".
  4. Agree on the reward before the week starts and write it on the sheet.
  5. Tick on the day, not at the end of the week, and print a fresh chart every Monday.

Frequently asked questions

Is the chore chart free?

Yes. The chore chart PDF is completely free. There is no signup and no email required. Click the download link and print a sheet for every week.

What does the chore chart template include?

Two A4 landscape pages. Page 1 is a household chart: 10 numbered chore rows, a Who column, and a checkbox for each day from Monday to Sunday, with a key and a line for a reward or notes. Page 2 is a chart per person: four cards, each with a name, a reward line, five chores by seven day boxes and a count of ticks for the week.

Which page should I use, the household chart or the one per person?

Use page 1 when one sheet on the fridge should show every chore and who does it. Use page 2 when each person, often each child, wants their own chart with their own reward. Many families print both: page 1 for the adults to share out the jobs, page 2 for the kids.

How is it different from the employee schedule?

The employee schedule is a work roster: each row is a person and each cell is a shift with a start and an end time. The chore chart is a list of jobs: each row is a chore, the Who column says who does it, and each day has a box to tick when it's done. For a team's working week, use the employee schedule instead.

What chores can kids do?

Pick chores they can finish alone. Younger children can make their bed, put toys away, feed a pet or set the table. Older children can empty the dishwasher, take out the bins, vacuum a room or fold their laundry. Shade the days a chore isn't due, so a blank box always means not done yet.

Can I print the chore chart on US Letter paper?

Yes. Both pages are A4 landscape with at least 13 mm of white space on every side, so they print on US Letter at Actual Size without clipping. If your printer still cuts an edge, choose "Fit to page" in the print dialog.
