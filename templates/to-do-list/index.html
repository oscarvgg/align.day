Why a to-do list needs a When column

A plain list grows faster than the day. It holds eight hours of tasks for three free hours, and the important ones sit next to the trivial ones. Two columns fix that. Minutes shows whether the list fits before you start: add them up and compare the total with your free time. When makes each task a decision instead of a hope, because a task with a start time is a time block, not a wish.

If you'd rather see the hours drawn out beside a shorter list, the daily planner template puts a 6 AM to 10 PM schedule and a 12-line to-do list on one page.

Not sure which tasks deserve a slot at all? Sort them first on the Eisenhower matrix template: do, schedule, delegate or drop.

How to turn a to-do list into time blocks

  1. Write down every task under Everything else, and how many hours you have free today in the Free time field.
  2. Circle a priority for each: A must happen today, B should, C could.
  3. Move three tasks to Must do. Pick the ones that would make the day a success, even if nothing else gets done.
  4. Guess the minutes and add them up on the Total line. More minutes than free time? Move the Cs to another day.
  5. Give each task a When. Must dos go first, into the hours when you think best. Batch small tasks into one slot instead of scattering them.
  6. Copy the times onto a schedule. For a full picture of the day, write each task into its slot on the daily time blocking template, around your meetings and meals.

Frequently asked questions

Is the to-do list template free?

Yes. The to-do list PDF is completely free. There is no signup and no email required. Download it and print as many pages as you need.

What is on the to-do list page?

One A4 page. At the top, a Date and a Free time field and a Must do box with three lines. Below it, 20 task lines. Every line has a Done box, the task, a priority to circle (A, B or C, or ! in Must do), a Minutes column and a When column for the start time. A Total line at the bottom adds up the minutes.

How is it different from the daily planner template?

The daily planner gives half the page to an hour-by-hour schedule and puts a 12-line to-do list beside it. The to-do list template is the whole page for tasks: 23 lines, each with a priority, its minutes and a When time, and no hour grid. Choose the daily planner if you want the hours drawn out, and this sheet if you start from a list and give each task a time.

Can I use it as a weekly to-do list?

Yes. Write the week's dates in the Date field and put a day and a time in the When column, such as Tue 9:00. The Must do box then holds the three tasks that matter most this week.

Can I print it on US Letter paper?

Yes. The page is A4 portrait with wide margins, so it prints on US Letter too. Choose "Fit to page" in the print dialog if your printer adds its own margins.

Can I use the to-do list on my iPad?

Yes. Open the PDF in GoodNotes, Notability, or any PDF annotation app and write on it with your Apple Pencil or finger.
