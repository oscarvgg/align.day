How to turn a month view into a plan

A month calendar shows what is fixed. A plan decides when everything else gets done. Use the month page for dates, and a weekly page for hours.

  1. Mark fixed dates first. Birthdays, trips, appointments and school days off, and the holidays already printed on the page.
  2. Write deadlines on the day they are due, then note a start date a few days before each one.
  3. Block the week. Each Sunday, copy the week's fixed dates into a weekly planner and give your priorities their hours: the undated Weekly Time Blocking Template fits any week, and the 2027 Weekly Planner is dated from the week of December 28, 2026.
  4. Review at the end of the month. Cross off what happened and carry what didn't into next month's page.
