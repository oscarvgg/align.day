How to plan 2027 with a printable calendar

A year calendar shows the shape of the year. A plan decides when the work happens. Use the year page to see busy stretches, the month pages for dates, and a weekly page for hours.

  1. Mark the year's fixed dates. Circle trips, school breaks, launches and family events on the year at a glance.
  2. Fill in each month before it starts. Copy its fixed dates into the day boxes and write deadlines on the day they are due.
  3. Plan the week. Each Sunday, move the week's dates into a weekly planner and give your priorities their hours.
  4. Review at the end of the month. Cross off what happened and carry what slipped into next month's page.
