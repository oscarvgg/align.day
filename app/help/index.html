Time blocks

  • Create and Edit a Time Block Create a time block in Align, set its title, time, calendar, color, icon and notes in the editor, and duplicate, lock or delete it from the selection menu.
  • Icon Suggestion Feature How Align's icon suggestion works: it reads the title you type for a time block and recommends a matching icon automatically.
  • Complete a Time Block Mark a time block as complete in Align from the timeline or the Timeline widget, undo it, and see how subtasks, repeating blocks and celebrations work.
  • Subtasks Plus Add checklist-style subtasks inside an Align time block, each with a title and completion state, to track the steps within one task.
  • Repeat a Time Block Set a time block in Align to repeat daily, weekly, monthly or yearly, choose when the series ends, and edit, move or delete one occurrence or the whole series.
  • Alarms and Notifications Add alarms to a time block in Align, allow notifications, and manage the daily review reminder.
  • Undo and Redo Undo and redo changes to your timeline in Align, such as moving, resizing, completing or deleting a time block, with gestures on iPhone or ⌘Z on iPad.

Arranging your day

  • Drag and Drop Move a time block in Align by tapping, holding and dragging it to a new time, with a live preview of where it lands before you release.
  • Resizing Time Blocks Resize a time block in Align by dragging either edge, adjusting its duration when a meeting runs long or finishes earlier than planned.
  • Overlap Avoidance Plus How Align's overlap avoidance shifts surrounding time blocks out of the way when you drag or resize one, so two blocks never occupy the same slot.
  • Locking Time Blocks Plus Lock a time block in Align so it cannot be dragged or pushed aside by other blocks - for meetings and classes that must stay exactly where they are.
  • Custom Day Start and End Plus Set the hours your Align day starts and ends, including a day that runs past midnight, and see how blocks outside those hours are handled.

Progress

  • Insights Plus See how your plan is going in Align Insights: pace for the day, what is still open, days cleared across a week or month, your streak, and a shareable image.
  • Widgets Add Align widgets to your Home Screen and Lock Screen to see your timeline, track today's progress, create time blocks and check your insights without opening the app.
