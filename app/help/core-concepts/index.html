Core Concepts

This page explains the main ideas behind Align. For step-by-step instructions, follow the links in each section or start with the Quick Start.

Time Blocking

Time blocking is a planning method where you give each task its own slot in your day. Instead of keeping a to-do list, you decide in advance when you will do each thing, and adjust the plan as the day changes.

Time Blocks

A time block is one planned task with a start and end time. Each block has a title and can also have:

  • A color and an icon
  • Alarms that notify you before it starts
  • A recurrence rule, so it repeats
  • Notes and, with Align Plus, subtasks

You mark a block as complete when you finish it.

The Timeline

The timeline is Align's main screen. It shows your time blocks for a day in time order, with the hours down the side and a date browser at the top. You move and resize blocks directly on the timeline.

Calendars Are the Source of Truth

Align doesn't keep a separate copy of your schedule. Every time block is an event in one of your device's calendars, such as iCloud or Google:

  • Events from the calendars you turn on appear as time blocks.
  • New time blocks are saved as events in your calendars.
  • Changes you make in another calendar app appear in Align, and changes in Align appear there.

Events from read-only calendars, such as subscribed calendars or shared calendars you can only view, appear as locked time blocks and can't be changed in Align.

A block's icon, color, subtasks and completion are Align's own data. They sync between your devices through iCloud, not through the calendar event.

Free and Align Plus
