Quick Start

This guide walks you through a first day with Align, from connecting your calendars to completing your first time block. Each step links to the page with the full details.

1. Connect Your Calendars

Align stores every time block as an event in your calendars, so it needs calendar access first.

  1. During onboarding, choose whether to bring your calendars:
    • Yes, bring my calendars: after you allow access, turn on the calendars you want in your timeline.
    • Keep Align separate: Align creates a calendar named "Align" for your time blocks.
  2. When iOS asks for permission, allow full access.
  3. To change which calendars appear later, open Settings → Calendars.

Depending on your version of onboarding, Align may ask for access without the Bring your calendars? question. See Set Up Your Calendars.

2. Create Your First Time Block

  1. Tap +, or tap Create time block in a gap on the timeline.
  2. Type a title.
  3. Tap Starts and Ends to set the time. A new block is 15 minutes long until you change its end.
  4. Optionally pick a Color, an icon and the Calendar to save it in.
  5. Tap Save (checkmark).

See Create and Edit a Time Block.

3. Set an Alarm

  1. Tap the time block on the timeline, then tap Edit.
  2. In the Alarms section, tap Add alarm. The first alarm is set to At time of event.
  3. Tap the alarm to choose another time, such as 15 minutes before.
  4. Save the time block.

If notifications aren't allowed yet, a message under Alarms lets you turn them on. See Alarms and Notifications.

4. Move or Resize It

Plans change during the day. Adjust the block on the timeline without opening the editor:

  • Move: touch and hold the block, drag it to a new time, then release. Drag it to the edge of the screen to move it to another day. See Drag and Drop.
  • Resize: tap the block to select it, then drag the handle on its top or bottom edge. See Resizing Time Blocks.

To read the timeline and move between days, see The Timeline.

5. Complete It

When you finish the block, tap its checkbox on the timeline. The title is struck through and dimmed. Tap the checkbox again to undo it.

See Complete a Time Block.

6. Check Your Progress

  • Widgets: add the Daily progress widget to see how many of today's blocks you've completed, or the Timeline widget to complete blocks from the Home Screen. See Widgets.
  • Insights (Align Plus): tap the chart icon on the timeline to review your day, week or month. See Insights.
