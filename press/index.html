About Align

One line

Align is a visual time blocking app for iPhone and iPad that turns tasks and calendar events into a realistic daily schedule.

Short boilerplate (about 50 words)

Align is a visual time blocking app for iPhone and iPad. It puts tasks and calendar events on one daily timeline, so people can see whether their plan fits the day and adjust it when things change. Align is free on the App Store, with an optional Align Plus subscription.

Long boilerplate (about 150 words)

Align is a visual daily planner for iPhone and iPad built around time blocking: giving each task its own slot in the day instead of leaving it on an open-ended to-do list. Tasks and events from the calendars already on the device appear together on one color-coded timeline, so it is clear at a glance how much of the day is planned and how much is free. Time blocks can be created, resized and moved, given colors and icons, and set to repeat. Align sends a reminder when a block is about to start, and Align Plus can keep blocks from overlapping. Data stays on the device and syncs through the user's own iCloud account. Align is built by Oscar Vicente Gonzalez Greco, an iOS developer with more than a decade of experience. It is free on the App Store, with an optional Align Plus subscription.

Usage Guidelines

Do

  • Write the name as "Align", with a capital A.
  • Use the app icon as supplied, at its original proportions, with some clear space around it.
  • Use the light icon on light backgrounds and the dark icon on dark backgrounds.
  • Link to align.day or the App Store listing when you mention the app.

Don't

  • Recolor, stretch, crop, rotate or add effects to the icon.
  • Combine the icon with other logos or use it to suggest a partnership or endorsement.
  • Create a new logo or wordmark for Align.
  • Edit screenshots to show features Align does not have.
