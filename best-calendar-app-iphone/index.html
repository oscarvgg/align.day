Best Calendar App for iPhone in 2026

Last updated: November 25, 2026. Align makes this page, and Align is one of the apps below. Facts about the other apps come from their own App Store listings and websites, read on that date.

To be clear from the start: Align is a day planner, not a full calendar app. It shows events from your iPhone calendars on its timeline, next to the time blocks you plan around them. If you need to send invitations, share calendars or manage several accounts, you want one of the calendar apps below, and you can use Align alongside it.

This page compares six calendar apps for iPhone and Align, with who each one suits, where it runs, and one real limitation.

Classic calendar apps

Pick one of these if your days are mostly events: meetings, appointments, invitations and shared family or work calendars.

Apple Calendar

Apple's Calendar app brings accounts from iCloud, Exchange, Google and others together, with List, Day, Week and Month views, invitations, shared calendars, and holidays and birthdays alongside your events (App Store).

  • Suits: anyone who wants a free calendar that's already on their iPhone.
  • Platforms: iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.
  • Limitation: it's made for Apple devices; the listing covers iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch only.

Google Calendar

Google Calendar adds reservations from Gmail to your calendar automatically, shows tasks next to events, and can show other calendars on your device, such as Exchange and iCloud (App Store, website).

  • Suits: people who already use Gmail or a Google account, and teams on Google Workspace.
  • Platforms: iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. Google's website also lists web and Android.
  • Limitation: some features require a Google Workspace or Google One AI Premium subscription.

Fantastical

Fantastical lets you type events and tasks in plain language, shows day, week, month, quarter and year views, and supports tasks from Todoist and Google Tasks (App Store, website).

  • Suits: people who want more views and faster entry than the built-in app.
  • Platforms: iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch on this listing. Flexibits' website also lists Mac, Windows and Apple Vision Pro, and says Android support is coming in 2027.
  • Limitation: scheduling by your availability, meeting polls, video call links and the extended weather forecast need Flexibits Premium, a subscription.

Microsoft Outlook

Outlook puts your email accounts and calendars in one app, and works with Exchange, Office 365, Outlook.com, Gmail, Yahoo and iCloud (App Store).

  • Suits: people who want email and calendar together, especially at work.
  • Platforms: iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. Microsoft also makes Outlook for Android.
  • Limitation: Copilot features need a Microsoft 365 Personal or Family subscription, or a business account with Copilot.

Calendar planner apps

Pick one of these if you want your events and your tasks in the same place.

Calendars by Readdle

Calendars by Readdle (listed as "Calendars: Schedule Planner") combines a calendar, a task manager, a daily planner and a habit tracker, with natural-language entry and sync with Google Calendar and iCloud (App Store, website).

  • Suits: people who want events, to-dos and routines in one app.
  • Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.
  • Limitation: the free plan is a basic calendar. Tasks, natural-language input, recurring events and use across devices need Calendars PRO, a subscription (free vs PRO).

Sorted³

Sorted³ puts your calendar events and tasks on one timeline, and its Auto Schedule helps you see what you can realistically get done in a day (App Store, website).

  • Suits: people who want to plan the whole day in one place.
  • Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.
  • Limitation: a core set of features is free, and the rest needs PRO, which is sold separately for iOS and macOS.

Align

Align is a day planner with a visual timeline. Events from the calendars on your iPhone show on the timeline, and you plan the open hours around them with time blocks: colors and icons, blocks that repeat, reminders, and overlap avoidance (in Align Plus) so blocks don't land on top of each other.

  • Suits: people who keep their calendar app for events and want a separate, visual plan for the rest of the day.
  • Platforms: iPhone and iPad, with iCloud sync between them.
  • Limitation: it's not a full calendar replacement: keep your calendar app for invitations and shared calendars. iPhone and iPad only, and there's no pomodoro timer, habit tracking, Live Activities or Focus modes.

Who should pick a classic calendar app instead of Align

Choose a classic calendar app such as Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Fantastical or Outlook instead of Align if:

  • Your day is mostly meetings and you need to send and answer invitations.
  • You share calendars with family or colleagues.
  • You also use devices other than Apple's. On an Android phone, pick Google Calendar or Outlook, which both have Android apps.

Choose Align as well if your calendar shows the meetings but not the work between them. Many people keep both: the calendar app holds events, and Align lays out the hours around them. Read more about how a time blocking app fits into the day.

How to choose a calendar app for iPhone

  1. Start with your accounts. If your calendars live in Google, Microsoft or iCloud, check that the app supports them.
  2. Decide whether you want tasks too. A classic calendar app holds events. A calendar planner app also puts tasks in your day.
  3. Check what's paid. Every app here is free to download. Several put features behind a subscription, so read the listing first.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best calendar app for iPhone?

For most people, the built-in Apple Calendar or Google Calendar covers events, invitations and shared calendars. Fantastical, Outlook and Calendars by Readdle add features such as natural-language entry, email in the same app, or tasks and habits. If you want to plan your open hours around your events, a day planner such as Align shows those events on a timeline next to your tasks.

Is Align a calendar app?

Align is a day planner, not a full calendar replacement. It shows events from the calendars on your iPhone on its timeline, next to your time blocks. Keep your calendar app for creating events, sending invitations and sharing calendars.

What is a calendar planner app?

A calendar planner app shows your calendar events and your tasks together, so you can see which hours are taken and plan the rest. Align, Sorted³ and Calendars by Readdle each combine events and tasks in different ways.
